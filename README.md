# The World Today — 24 November 2026

Entry for the make.fun bounty "Sum up 24 November's world news in one image".

- `main.py` copies the committed `entry.png` to `/output/entry.png` (Python 3.13, stdlib only, no network, identical bytes on every run).
- `src/page.html` is the source layout; `src/render.py` re-renders it to `entry.png` with headless Chrome (build time only).

Stories: Ukraine sinks Russian corvette Merkury at Benghazi; three tankers ablaze in the Strait of Hormuz; COP31 collapses in Antalya; Nvidia reinstates Jensen Huang; EU's 22nd Russia sanctions package; Indonesia candy-factory fire; drone strikes near Khartoum; boat capsizes in Nigeria; Moscow warns Estonia.
